## What the four share
- One quiet ground (cream or off-white) and mostly one ink. Colour is rare and means something.
- A serif voice for reading and display; small caps or mono for metadata.
- Hand-made marks sit *on top of* a structure that would still work without them.
- The marks point at, frame or caption real content (a photo, an object, a word). They are never filler.
